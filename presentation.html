Context & Ground Reality

Disaster Bottlenecks in Monsoon Flood Response

🌧️ Optical Satellites Fail in Monsoons

Traditional optical satellites (Sentinel-2, Landsat, MODIS) cannot penetrate dense monsoon cloud cover and heavy rainfall. In peak floods, rescue authorities are left completely blind for days.

🚗 Standard Navigation Traps Rescue Teams

Commercial routing platforms (Google Maps, Apple Maps) lack real-time inundation boundaries. They blindly navigate relief trucks and ambulances straight into submerged highways and washed-out bridges.

⏳ 6–8 Hour Manual GIS Processing Delays

Existing disaster GIS workflows require manual satellite downloading, calibration, and human polygon tracing. By the time maps reach ground commanders, floodwaters have already shifted.

📦 Disconnected Mutual-Aid Logistics

Grassroots volunteers with boats and food supplies have no spatial visibility into where cut-off victims are stranded. SOS calls on social media lack verified geographic coordinates and hazard contexts.

Nishtha Patel (CSE #1) — Backend & Satellite Pipeline

SAR Radar Physics & Automated Data Pipeline

📡 Radar Reflection Physics & Specular Attenuation
  • All-Weather C-Band SAR: Sentinel-1 emits 5.405 GHz microwave pulses that completely ignore clouds, monsoon rain, and darkness.
  • Specular Surface Reflection: Dry ground and forest create rough diffuse scatter (high backscatter dB: -12 to -8 dB). Smooth standing water reflects radar pulses away like a mirror (low backscatter dB: -22 to -18 dB).
  • Change Detection Algorithm: Ratio between pre-flood baseline (29 June 2024) and peak inundation (11 July 2024) isolates exact flooded pixels with morphological speckle filtering.
⚙️ Automated Semi-Live Ingestion & Export Engine
  • Programmatic Copernicus Ingestion: ingest_sentinel1.py queries OData catalogue for IW_GRDH_1SDV products over the AOI [90.64, 25.09, 93.16, 26.70] with OAuth2 credentials and open fallback.
  • State Tracking Manifest: data/manifest.json compares scene timestamps to skip redundant processing during the 6–12 day orbital revisit cycle.
  • Vectorized GeoJSON Export: detect_flood.py automatically converts cleaned binary raster masks into EPSG:4326 WGS84 GeoJSON polygons with total area calculations (723.94 km² active extent).

Mahathi (CSE #2) — Spatial Routing & Optimization

Hazard-Aware Safe Route Finder Engine

🛡️ Dynamic Flood Avoidance Algorithm
  • 1. Point-in-Polygon Check: Instantly evaluates if origin or destination hospital is submerged in floodwaters.
  • 2. Multi-Candidate OSRM Pathfinding: Fetches primary and alternative highway geometries via Open Source Routing Machine.
  • 3. 4-Directional Bypass Detour: If primary road is cut by water, automatically tests North/South/East/West elevated bypass waypoints (~5km buffer) to find a dry corridor.
⚡ Spatial BBOX Indexing (4.6x Speedup)
  • The Bottleneck Solved: Testing candidate routes against 221 complex flood polygons across Assam used to take seconds and freeze the browser.
  • Spatial BBOX Pre-Filtering (< 2ms): Pre-computes [minX, minY, maxX, maxY] for each polygon. Fast O(1) rectangle overlap rejects 98% of distant polygons instantly.
  • Timeout Guards & Memory LRU Cache: Concurrent Promise.all detour queries, strict 4.5s timeout with emergency vector fallback, and sub-millisecond route caching.

GIS Multi-Criteria Risk Modeling & Infrastructure Exposure

📊 Multi-Criteria Hazard Scoring (QGIS)

Risk = w_f·(Flood Depth) + w_h·(1 / Dist_Hospital) + w_r·(Road Severance)

  • 🔴 High Risk: 272.15 km² (41.22%) — Submerged areas within 2km of hospitals or blocking main arterial highways.
  • 🟠 Medium Risk: 190.55 km² (28.86%) — Flooded secondary agricultural and local road networks.
  • 🟢 Low Risk: 197.55 km² (29.92%) — Inundated open floodplains far from human settlement.
